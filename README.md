Ship Fuel Efficiency and Emissions Analysis Report

1. Introduction

This report presents an analysis of ship operational performance using Power BI. The objective of the analysis is to evaluate fuel consumption, engine efficiency, distance traveled, and carbon dioxide (CO₂) emissions across different ship types. The dashboard provides interactive visualizations that support decision-making related to operational efficiency, fuel management, and environmental sustainability.

---

2. Dashboard Overview

The dashboard includes the following key performance indicators (KPIs):

* Total Fuel Consumption: 6.98 Million
* Total CO₂ Emissions: 19.25 Million
* Average Engine Efficiency: 82.58%
* Total Distance Travelled: 218,524.83 units

The dashboard also includes visualizations for engine efficiency, fuel consumption, distance traveled, CO₂ emissions, and fuel-type filtering.

---

3. Engine Efficiency Analysis

The engine efficiency chart compares the performance of different ship types.

Findings

* Oil Service Boats demonstrate the highest engine efficiency.
* Tanker Ships closely follow Oil Service Boats in efficiency.
* Surfer Boats show moderate efficiency levels.
* Fishing Trawlers exhibit the lowest engine efficiency among all ship categories.

Interpretation

Higher engine efficiency indicates better fuel utilization and operational performance. The results suggest that Oil Service Boats and Tanker Ships are more optimized for fuel usage, while Fishing Trawlers may require maintenance or technological improvements to improve efficiency.

---

4. Fuel Consumption Analysis

The fuel consumption visualizations (bar chart and pie chart) illustrate the total fuel consumed by each ship type.

Findings

* Tanker Ships account for the highest fuel consumption, consuming approximately 4.4 million units.
* Oil Service Boats and Fishing Trawlers consume moderate amounts of fuel.
* Surfer Boats have the lowest fuel consumption.

Interpretation

The significantly higher fuel consumption of Tanker Ships can be attributed to their larger size and operational requirements. Since Tanker Ships contribute the majority of fuel usage, fuel optimization initiatives should focus primarily on this category to reduce operational costs.

---

## 5. Distance Travelled Analysis

The distance summary table displays the cumulative distance covered by each ship type.

Findings

* Tanker Ships traveled the greatest distance.
* Oil Service Boats, Fishing Trawlers, and Surfer Boats covered comparatively shorter distances.

Interpretation

The high distance traveled by Tanker Ships explains their greater fuel consumption levels. Distance traveled is a key operational factor influencing fuel requirements and overall emissions.

---

6. CO₂ Emissions Analysis

The monthly CO₂ emissions chart evaluates environmental impact throughout the year.

Findings

* CO₂ emissions remain relatively stable across all months.
* Minor fluctuations are observed, but no extreme peaks or declines are present.
* Total CO₂ emissions reached approximately 19.25 million units.

Interpretation

The consistency in emissions suggests stable operational activity throughout the year. Since fuel consumption directly influences emissions, reducing fuel usage can contribute significantly to environmental sustainability.

---

7. Fuel Type Filter Analysis

The dashboard includes a fuel-type slicer that allows users to analyze ships operating with:

* Diesel
* Heavy Fuel Oil (HFO)

This feature enables detailed comparisons of operational performance and environmental impact across different fuel sources.

---

8. Key Insights

1. Tanker Ships are the largest consumers of fuel and contribute the highest operational activity.
2. Oil Service Boats exhibit the best engine efficiency among all ship types.
3. Fishing Trawlers show the lowest efficiency and may benefit from optimization measures.
4. Total fleet fuel consumption reached approximately 6.98 million units.
5. Total fleet CO₂ emissions reached approximately 19.25 million units.
6. Emissions remain relatively consistent throughout the year.
7. Fuel efficiency improvements can significantly reduce operating costs and environmental impact.

---

9. Conclusion

The analysis demonstrates that ship type plays a significant role in fuel consumption, engine efficiency, and emissions. Tanker Ships dominate overall operational activity and fuel usage, while Oil Service Boats achieve the highest efficiency levels. The dashboard provides valuable insights for monitoring fleet performance, reducing fuel costs, and supporting sustainability initiatives. Future analysis may include predictive modeling, maintenance optimization, and fuel-efficiency forecasting to further improve operational effectiveness.
